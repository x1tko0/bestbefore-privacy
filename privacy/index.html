BestBefore

Expiry Tracker — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your shelf stays on your phone. BestBefore has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission. Barcodes and dates are read on your device.

Data the app stores

What you enter or scan: item names, barcodes, categories, storage places, expiry and opening dates, quantities, optional prices, notes, item photos you take, the list of products the app remembers, and app settings.

  • Stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Never sent to the developer or any third party.
  • Deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes and printed dates and to take item photos. Recognition runs entirely on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device models. Camera frames are not saved or sent anywhere; only photos you choose to take are stored, in the app's private storage.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include BestBefore's data (without photos) in your device backup to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by Android and Google (Google Privacy Policy), not by us, and you can turn it off in system settings.
  • Backup file. “Export backup” and “Export CSV” create a file only where you choose to save it. We never see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, or files (except a backup file you pick to import).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app on your phone:

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “BestBefore Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details.
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by Google Play.

Notifications and widgets

The morning summary is scheduled on your device. Home screen widgets show the names of items that expire soon — anyone who can see your screen can see them.

Not advice

BestBefore reminds you of dates you enter. It does not decide whether food or medicine is safe to use; always follow the label.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
